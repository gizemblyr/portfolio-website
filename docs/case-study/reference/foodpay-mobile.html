01 Overview

Design Needs and Focus

FoodPay (YemeksepetiPay) is a payment and virtual POS platform designed from scratch to let businesses receive online payments securely, operating under TCMB regulations. From day one, the product was shaped with a strong UX foundation that took regulatory requirements, security and financial operations into account.

Core design needs

  • Build a secure, regulation-compliant payment infrastructure for merchants
  • Establish trust at the very first point of contact in a fintech product
  • Make security, verification and error scenarios clear and understandable
  • Present critical financial information such as payments, settlements and reports in a simple, structured way
  • Create a scalable, consistent UI structure for a product built from scratch

Based on these needs, the entire experience was handled end to end, from the landing page and merchant onboarding to the dashboard and reporting screens.

03 Research

Research and Mini Benchmark

Since the product was built from scratch, a mini benchmark study was conducted to understand industry standards.

Platforms reviewed

  • iyzico
  • Stripe
  • PayPal

Focus areas

  • Merchant onboarding and verification flows
  • Document upload and approval processes
  • How security and regulatory messages are communicated

The analysis showed that long, mandatory processes can be managed effectively through clear guidance, progress visibility and well-crafted microcopy.

04 Wireframes

Wireframes

Wireframe work covered the key flows:

  • Landing page structure and navigation
  • Sign-up and login flows
  • Merchant onboarding: authorised person details, business info, document upload, agreements
  • Dashboard, transaction summaries and reporting screens

A primary goal during onboarding was to clearly communicate where the user is in the process and what comes next.

05 Design system

A Compact Design System for the MVP

Standardised components

Form fields, validation states and error messages were standardised across all flows.

Visual hierarchy

Consistent typography and hierarchy were defined for presenting financial data.

Reusable patterns

Reusable components for security and status messaging, designed to support future growth.

06 UI Design

UI Screens

07 Impact

Impact and Key Learnings

Impact

  • An end-to-end first product experience was delivered for FoodPay
  • A regulation-compliant, trust-focused and scalable UI foundation was established
  • Complex financial processes were made manageable from a user perspective

Key learnings

  • In financial products, UX affects not only users but also operational and support teams
  • Poorly structured onboarding increases long-term support load and operational costs
  • Trust is not built on a single screen, but through consistent, coherent flows across the whole experience
